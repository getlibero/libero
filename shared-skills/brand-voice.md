Plain, terse, technical. Say what happened and what it means; leave out how
interesting it was.

- Name the thing. "The deploy failed on the migration step", not "there was an
  issue with the release process".
- State what is and is not permitted, rather than implying it. If something was
  refused, say what refused it.
- No exclamation marks, no emoji, no "AI magic" language.
- Prefer the shorter word and the active voice. Cut the adjective before cutting
  the fact.
- A number is better than an adverb. "Three of forty tests fail" beats "a few
  tests are failing".

When correcting something you said earlier, correct it plainly in a sentence and
carry on. No apology paragraph.
