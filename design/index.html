The app icon is the mark knocked out of a filled --lb-accent rounded square, radius 17/64, and it never goes light — one lockup everywhere. Below 20px the dot is dropped, the corner opens, and the stroke thickens so the form survives.

RULE · ELEVATION

Dark lifts a surface by getting lighter (canvas → surface → raised). Light lifts by gaining a border — white on #F7F9F8 plus a hairline. No shadows in either mode.

RULE · THE BRAND DOESN'T MOVE

#1BA85A is the green in both modes — picked to clear 3:1 against #0B0F0E and against white, so the mark, filled buttons, and dots never change. Only --lb-accent-text shifts, and only where green is set as type.

RULE · RESTRAINT

Green means allowed and executed, amber means awaiting a human, red means blocked. Nothing else on screen is coloured.

06

Handoff

Dark on :root, light behind [data-theme="light"]. 
<link rel="stylesheet" href="tokens.css">   <!-- the pairs, nothing else -->
<link rel="stylesheet" href="libero.css">   <!-- type, components, elevation -->

<html>                        dark — the default, no attribute needed
<html data-theme="light">     light — a peer, not an inversion

Three things don't switch: the brand green, the ink that sits on it (--lb-accent-ink, always #06120B — so an Approve button is pixel-identical in both modes), and the type stack. Amber and red are still tuned per mode: they're status, not identity, and nobody recognises a product by its warning colour.
